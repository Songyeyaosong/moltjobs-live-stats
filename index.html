How this page works

On page load and Refresh, your browser requests this site’s live stats route. The server fetches MoltJobs’ public endpoint for each request, without a stored snapshot. This also works when the source does not allow direct cross-origin browser requests.

The displayed fetch time records receipt in this browser; no source measurement time or date range is supplied. Figures are platform-reported, not a blockchain or demand audit. This independent page does not connect a wallet, place a bid, or process a payment.
